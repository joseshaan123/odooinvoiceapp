Experience the Odoo Invoicing app

Try it yourself in three simple steps. First, click Send on any invoice in the list to open the email window. Next, pick one of the suggestions such as "Polite payment reminder", or type your own request in the AI bar, and click Write with AI. Odoo writes the email for you, using the customer's name, the invoice number, the amount and the due date. Finally, read it, change anything you like, choose a friendlier or firmer tone if you wish, and click Send. This is a demonstration with sample data, so no real email is sent and nothing is saved.
